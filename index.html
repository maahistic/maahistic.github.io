01 / about

Engineer first. Lead by example.

Java Full Stack Module Lead with a track record of leading Agile teams that deliver scalable microservices and middleware with Spring Boot, Apache Camel and Angular.

My day-to-day sits where reliability matters most: distributed caching with Hazelcast, messaging with Kafka, ActiveMQ and IBM MQ, cloud-native services on AWS, and Docker-based delivery. I care about the unglamorous parts that keep production calm — CI/CD automation in GitLab, test depth with JUnit 5 and mutation testing, SonarQube quality gates and well-documented APIs with Swagger.

As a lead I practise SAFe Agile, run story grooming and estimation, and turn business goals into technical designs the team can ship with confidence.

  • Reliability over clevernessIdempotent flows, retries and observability by default.
  • Tests as a contractUnit, integration and mutation tests guard every change.
  • Teams that scaleClear stories, honest estimates, shared ownership.
